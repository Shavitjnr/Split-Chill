Every Feature Engineered To
Eliminate Awkward Money Talks

Never calculate tip percentages on napkins again. Spill Chill takes messy receipts and turns them into crystal-clear micro-balances.

AI Receipt Scanner (OCR)

Snap a photo of any restaurant receipt, grocery bill, or handwritten bar tab. Our multi-modal vision engine isolates line items, taxes, and service fees in 1.4 seconds.

Mathematical Debt Minimizer

Why send 15 peer transfers when 2 can settle the entire trip? Our graph optimization algorithm finds the mathematical minimum transaction paths across any group size.

160+ Multi-Currency Conversions

Travelling across borders? Log in Euros, Yen, Pesos, or USD. Instant spot-rate normalization ensures nobody loses money to hidden foreign exchange fees.

Automated Recurring Bills

Set and forget rent, Wi-Fi, Netflix, cleaning supplies, and utility invoices. Spill Chill automatically logs and assigns scheduled monthly splits on the 1st of every month.

Zero-Knowledge Privacy

We never sell your spending habits or financial footprints to advertisers. Self-hostable, end-to-end encrypted, and compliant with strict global financial privacy standards.

One-Click CSV / PDF Audits

Download comprehensive balance sheets, itemized breakdowns, and transaction history receipts ready for accountant review, roommates, or tax submissions.
